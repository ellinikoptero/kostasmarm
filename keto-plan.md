# Keto Structure — 120 → 80 kg | 2,500 kcal

## 1. Daily Targets

| Metric | Target | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Calories | **2,500 kcal** | Maintenance ~3,250 (BMR 2,290 × 1.4) − 22% |
| Protein | **160 g** (640 kcal) | 2 g/kg × 80 kg target weight |
| Net carbs | **≤ 25 g** (100 kcal) | Hard ceiling |
| Fat | **~195 g** (1,755 kcal) | Fills remaining calories — a ceiling, not a goal |

Expected rate: ~0.7–0.9 kg/week after the week-1 water drop (2–4 kg).

## 2. Meal Template — 3 slots, rotate freely

Portion logic: protein = 2 palm-sized servings (~200–250 g cooked) per main meal · olive oil measured by the tablespoon (1 tbsp = 14 g fat) · vegetables unlimited if green/leafy.

## 4. Electrolytes — Weeks 1–3 (non-negotiable)

| Electrolyte | Daily amount | How |
|---|---|---|
| Sodium | 4–5 g (≈ 2–2.5 tsp salt) | Salt all food + ½ tsp in a glass of water morning & pre-gym |
| Potassium | 3–4 g | Food first: avocado, horta, yogurt; or "No Salt"–type substitute (KCl) |
| Magnesium | 300–400 mg | Supplement, glycinate or citrate, taken evening |

Headache, fatigue, or cramps in weeks 1–2 = electrolytes, not hunger. Fix salt first.

## 5. Adjustment Rules

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Scale stalls ≥ 2 weeks (7-day averages flat) | First audit hidden carbs & portions for 3 days. If clean: cut fat by 25 g (−225 kcal → ~170 g). Protein and carbs unchanged. Never cut protein. |
| Lifts drop 2 sessions in a row | First: +1 extra ½ tsp salt 60 min pre-training + full sleep check. If still weak: add 20–25 g fast carbs immediately pre-workout only (targeted keto) — total daily carbs may reach ~45 g on training days. |

## 6. Weekly Check — same day, every week

| ✔ | Metric | Method |
|---|---|---|
| ☐ | Weight trend | Weigh daily after waking; log **7-day average**; compare average vs last week — ignore daily jumps |
| ☐ | Waist | At navel, relaxed, morning, before food — once per week |
| ☐ | Key lifts | Top set weight × reps: squat, bench, row (or your program's equivalents) |
| ☐ | Adherence | Days on plan /7 · electrolytes taken /7 |

Decision rule: waist ↓ or lifts steady = working, even if scale is flat that week.
